🔴 ALERT

That "Invoice Attached" Email Is a Trap. You get an email that looks like it's from a vendor, DocuSign, or even a colleague: "Invoice #12345 attached — please review and pay." The attachment is a PDF or Word doc. You open it, and nothing seems to happen. But in the background, a script downloads malware from Discord's CDN or a fake login page steals your credentials. This campaign has been running since late 2025 and hits businesses and individuals alike. The emails use real branding, spoofed sender addresses, and urgent language ("past due," "final notice"). What to do: Don't open unexpected invoice attachments. If you weren't expecting an invoice, call the sender at a known number — not one in the email. Hover over links to see the real URL. Report phishing to your IT person or forward to reportphishing@apwg.org. *Source: BleepingComputer, MalwareTips, GridinSoft — July 2026*

Daily Tip

Every open program eats memory and slows your computer down. Look at your taskbar (bottom of screen) — see those little dots under the icons? Each one is a running program. Right-click any you're not using right now and choose "Close window." Your computer will feel snappier instantly, especially if you only have 8GB of RAM or less.
