🔴 ALERT

The Federal Trade Commission is warning about scammers cloning real car-dealership websites. They copy the logo, car photos, and customer reviews, then take an upfront deposit for a vehicle that doesn't exist. Buyers show up at the lot and find no record of their order or money. Before paying: search the dealer's name plus the word "scam," and insist on seeing the car in person or having an independent inspector check it. Never send a deposit by wire transfer. Report fake dealer sites at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. (Source: FTC, Sept. 1, 2026)

Daily Tip

Stop hunting through menus for your programs. Press the Start button and just start typing its name — "Calculator," "Photos," or "Excel." The program you want appears at the top of the list; press Enter and it opens. Free, built into Windows, and faster than clicking through folders.
