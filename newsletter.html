Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Microsoft pulled a faulty update that switched off Word and Excel on home computers — people opened a document and were told their software was unlicensed, or found it gone. One repair shop had five customers hit within ten minutes, and I've had calls here too. Don't pay to fix it, and be wary of any message offering to do it for you — that's where the scam starts. Open Word or Excel, click Reactivate, and sign in with the account you normally use; if it's gone, reinstall the copy you bought. Source: BleepingComputer, September 26 — Microsoft confirmed it paused the update, according to its support notes.

Daily Tip

Press the Windows key plus the left arrow, and the window you're using jumps to the left half of your screen. Do the same with the right arrow on another window — now they sit side by side.
