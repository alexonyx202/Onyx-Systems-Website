Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

A fake CAPTCHA pops up and tells you to press the Windows key + R, paste something, and hit Enter. Don't do it. That "verification" is a trap — it pastes malicious code into Windows Run and executes it. Real CAPTCHAs never ask for keyboard commands. Ever. What to do: Close the browser tab immediately. If the tab won't close, hold your power button for 15 seconds until the screen goes black, then turn it back on. You'll be fine. I've seen this trick catch people off guard because it looks like a normal "prove you're human" box. The giveaway: no legitimate site asks you to open Run or paste commands. Source: Malwarebytes Labs — September 2026
