🔴 ALERT

Searching Google for a Windows app? The first result might be a trap. Security researchers found over **70 fake websites** impersonating popular Windows programs — VLC, Notepad++, 7-Zip, WinRAR, and more. They buy Google ads so they appear *above* the real site. Click "Download" and you get malware that steals passwords, mines crypto, or locks your files. The real sites: `videolan.org`, `notepad-plus-plus.org`, `7-zip.org`, `win-rar.com`. Bookmark them. Or get apps from the Microsoft Store. If a download page asks you to run an `.exe` named "setup_vlc_3.0.20.exe" from a domain like `vlc-media-player-download.com`, **close the tab**. *Source: Windows Latest — July 27, 2026*

Daily Tip

Hold **Ctrl** and tap **+** (plus) to zoom in. **Ctrl** and **−** (minus) zooms out. **Ctrl** and **0** resets to normal. Works in your browser, email, File Explorer, PDFs — basically everywhere. Your eyes will thank you.
