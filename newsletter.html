🔴 ALERT

The FTC just warned that scammers are buying fake ads at the top of Google and Bing that look like your electric company, insurance provider, or bank. You search for "pay my light bill," click the first result, enter your account number and card, and the money goes to a crook instead of your real company. Always scroll past the ads and look for the official web address you already know — or call the number on your paper bill. Report fraud at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. (Source: FTC.gov, August 17, 2026)

Daily Tip

If your screen looks like a flashlight in a dark room, your eyes are paying the price. Headaches and tired eyes at the end of the day often come from a screen that's too bright for the lighting around you. On a Windows computer, click the battery icon in the bottom-right corner and slide the brightness bar until the screen feels comfortable — like reading a book, not staring into a lamp. Your eyes will thank you by lunchtime.
