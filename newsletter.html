💡 Daily Tip

Give Your Browser Extensions a Cleanup

Most browser hijacks ride in through extensions you forgot you installed. Open Chrome → Extensions → Manage Extensions and remove anything you don't recognize or no longer use. Stick to tools from known developers — a free "PDF converter" can quietly siphon your saved passwords. Takes about two minutes, twice a year.
