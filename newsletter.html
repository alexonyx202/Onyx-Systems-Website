Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Your Router Is the Front Door — Is It Locked? Most people never change their router's default admin password. That's like leaving your front door wide open with a sign that says "admin/admin." Hackers scan for these constantly. Once inside, they can redirect your banking, spy on everything, or lock you out entirely. What to do: Log into your router (usually 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1 in your browser). Change the admin password to something only you know. Save it. Done. If you've never done this, assume the door is open. *Source: CISA.gov — "Secure Your Home Network" (updated 2026)*

Daily Tip

Unplug the power from BOTH your modem and your router. Wait 30 full seconds — count them. Plug the modem in first, wait for all its lights to go solid (usually 1-2 minutes). THEN plug in the router. This clears the connection handshake properly and fixes most "internet is slow but connected" issues.
