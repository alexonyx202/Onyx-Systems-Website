Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Those little boxes websites use to check "Are you human?" — scammers are putting up fakes. They ask you to press the Windows key and R, paste a code, and press Enter "to verify." What really happens is a hidden command runs that can sneak a bad program in or open a doorway into your computer. Microsoft and the FTC are warning about this trick right now. A real human check never asks you to run anything. If you see that, close the tab. *Source: Microsoft Security warning (Aug. 28) with fresh reports Sept. 2–3, 2026; FTC Consumer Alerts*

Daily Tip

Keep a copy of any document or web page as a PDF — no printer needed. Open the item, click File, then Print (or hold the Ctrl key and press P), choose "Microsoft Print to PDF" as the printer, and click Save. Receipts, forms, articles — now they're tidy files on your computer, ready to email or file away.
