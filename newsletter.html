Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

A hacker broke into ASOS, the big online clothing store, and walked off with customer information. The company confirmed the break-in this week after shoppers started seeing strange messages. If you have an ASOS account, change that password today — and never reuse it anywhere else. Crooks who steal one password try it on your email and your bank next. I've had customers lose an account for exactly this reason. Two minutes now saves a world of grief. Source: ASOS breach confirmation reported by BleepingComputer (Oct. 7, 2026).

Daily Tip

Add a second lock to your email. Open your email in a web browser, find Security in the settings, and turn on two-step verification. From then on a one-time code is sent to you, so a stolen password alone can't get in. Takes about two minutes.
