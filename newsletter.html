Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

A new bad program called AmnesiaStealer is spreading through fake "download" pages. The page tells you to copy a command and paste it — and once it's in, it takes over the program you use to go on the internet and can steal your saved passwords. Real download pages never ask you to copy and paste commands. If one does, close the tab. Only download programs from the maker's real website, and keep your computer's free updates turned on. *Source: BleepingComputer — August 16, 2026*

Daily Tip

Pick the one folder you'd hate to lose — your photos or your work papers. Plug in a USB drive, copy that folder onto it, and put the drive somewhere safe. Do it once a week. A dead computer stops being a disaster when you've got a spare copy. Two minutes, once a week.
