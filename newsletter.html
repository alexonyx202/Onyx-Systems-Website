🔴 ALERT

A package shows up on your porch you never ordered. The FTC warned on August 20 that this is a "brushing" scam — sellers send you free junk using your name so they can post a fake five-star review in your name. Some packages include QR codes that lead to fake websites designed to steal your information. If you receive a package you did not order, do not scan any codes inside. Check your online accounts for unfamiliar orders and report it free at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. *Source: Federal Trade Commission — August 20, 2026*

Today in Tech

  • **Windows 11's August update is crashing games** — Microsoft confirmed the security patch released August 11 is breaking some PC games and causing random restarts. If games stopped working, uninstall the update at Settings > Windows Update > Update History (BleepingComputer, August 20)
  • **On Q Financial agrees to $1.25 million settlement** after a breach exposed Social Security numbers and financial data of over 3,000 customers. If you had an account with them, check your mail for a settlement notice and consider a free credit freeze (ClassAction, August 21)

Daily Tip

Press the Windows key and type "Calendar" to open the free app built into your computer. Click any date, type what you need to remember, and set a reminder time — it pops up on your screen even if you are not looking at the calendar. Use it for doctor appointments, bill due dates, or when to renew your car tags.
