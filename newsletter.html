Daily Tip

Set up "emergency access" in Bitwarden, 1Password, or Proton Pass. Designate a trusted contact who can request access to your vault if you're incapacitated — they get in after a waiting period you set (e.g., 7 days), during which you can deny the request if you're fine. It's the digital equivalent of a spare key with a trusted neighbor. Takes two minutes to configure. Zero cost on free tiers.
