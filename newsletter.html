Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Scammers are buying the ads at the top of search results when you look up a bill — power, water, or internet. You click a fake payment page that looks just like your provider's, pay it, and the money goes straight to a crook while your real bill sits unpaid. I check the address bar and type my provider's exact website myself instead of trusting the first ad. Pay only through the bill you were mailed, and report fakes at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Source: FTC Consumer Advice and Yahoo News — September 24, 2026
