Daily Tip

Your computer might be slow because too many programs are running without you knowing. Press Ctrl+Shift+Esc to open Task Manager, then click "More details" if you see a simple view. Click the "Startup" tab to see which programs start automatically when you turn on your computer. Right-click any program you don't need at startup and select "Disable." This can shave minutes off your boot time and free up memory for the things you actually use.
