🔴 ALERT

Police put out a fresh warning this week that gift-card scams are going after older adults and draining savings. Here is a rule that never fails: no real bank, sheriff's office, insurance company, or store will ever ask you to pay a bill with gift cards. Once you read the numbers off the back, the money is gone and there is no refund. If a caller keeps you on the line and pushes you to go buy cards, hang up — I've never once seen a real bill that worked that way. Source: Heber City Police Department warning (Sept. 28, 2026), reported by KPCW; payment rules confirmed by the FTC's official guidance at ftc.gov, according to its consumer page.
