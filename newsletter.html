🔴 ALERT

A Florida "ransomware recovery" company just got charged by the Department of Justice for taking victims' money, paying the hackers a fraction of it, and keeping the rest. MonsterCloud told people it had "proprietary tools" to decrypt files — but court documents say they had no such technology. One client paid $150,000; the hackers got $8,200. The owner pocketed the difference. The rule: nobody can magically unlock ransomware without the key. If a company claims they can, they're either lying or paying the criminals themselves. Real recovery means restoring from a clean backup. What to do: if ransomware hits, don't panic-call a recovery firm. Disconnect the computer, call a local tech you trust, and restore from backup. Report it to IC3.gov. Source: U.S. Department of Justice press release, October 8, 2026; TechSpot, October 8, 2026.

Daily Tip

Restart your computer once a week. It clears memory leaks, unsticks pending updates, and fixes the sluggish feeling that builds up over days of sleep/wake cycles. Click Start → Power → Restart. Takes 30 seconds and your machine will thank you.
