Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

The Federal Trade Commission warns that ads on social media platforms are not checked for scams. Crooks run fake ads on Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok using stolen product photos and made-up reviews — you pay but the item never arrives, or it shows up broken. The FTC says this happens every day and costs people real money (FTC.gov, August 2026). Before buying from any social media ad, search the company name plus "scam" on Google. If the company has no real website or phone number, don't buy. Always use a credit card so you can dispute the charge.

  • The FBI, CISA, and health officials updated their warning about Medusa, a nasty program that locks up hospital and business computers for ransom — it has now hit over 500 organizations since 2021 (CISA.gov, August 18).
