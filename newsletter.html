🔴 ALERT

Apple rushed out an emergency update this week for a flaw hackers are already using against Apple computers. The fix stops a harmful file — one you might open from an email or a download — from running hidden code. The real repair is free from Apple, so ignore any pop-up telling you to call a number; that is a scam. It takes two minutes: open the Apple menu, click System Settings, then General, then Software Update. I've seen computers sit months behind — please update today.
