🔴 ALERT

Google just pushed an emergency Chrome update fixing 37 security holes — seven of them rated Critical. These are memory-corruption bugs that let a malicious website run code on your computer just by you visiting the page. No clicks needed. The affected versions are Chrome 155 before 155.0.8059.79 on Windows/Mac and 155.0.8059.79 on Linux. What to do: Click the three-dot menu (top-right in Chrome) > Help > About Google Chrome. Chrome will check and install the update automatically. Relaunch when it asks. Takes 30 seconds. The rule: Your web browser is the #1 way bad code gets in. Keeping it updated is the single most effective thing you can do. Source: Malwarebytes, Windows Forum, Cybersecurity News — October 13–14, 2026

Daily Tip

A print job frozen in the queue blocks everything behind it. Click the Start button, type "printers," and open Printers & scanners. Click your printer, then Open queue. Right-click the stuck job and choose Cancel. If it won't clear, restart the Print Spooler: press Start, type "services," open Services, find Print Spooler, right-click and Restart.
