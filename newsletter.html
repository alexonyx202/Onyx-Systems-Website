🔴 ALERT

An official-looking plastic Medicare card is landing in mailboxes, with a phone number to "activate" your new benefits — and that call is the whole scam. Real Medicare cards are paper, and nobody from Medicare will ever call, text, or email asking for your Medicare, Social Security, or bank number. Don't ring a number printed on the letter — type medicare.gov into your browser yourself, or use 800-633-4227, the number I check before I trust it. Already shared it? Report it at ftc.gov. Source: FTC consumer alert, "What to know ahead of Open Enrollment to avoid health insurance scams" (Sept. 28, 2026), according to the agency's official page at ftc.gov; Senior Medicare Patrol warning on plastic Medicare cards, reported by Saving Advice, Sept. 28, 2026.
