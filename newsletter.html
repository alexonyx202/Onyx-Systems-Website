Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Microsoft pushed a fresh security patch to Edge this week that closes several vulnerabilities attackers are actively exploiting through malicious websites. If your Edge browser is out of date, simply visiting the wrong page could let someone run code on your computer. Here's the fix — it's free and takes 30 seconds: open Microsoft Edge, click the three dots in the top right corner, choose Help and feedback, then About Microsoft Edge. The browser will show your current version and update automatically if it's not current. I do this check on every computer that comes into the shop, and I'm always surprised how many are months behind. Don't be one of them — check it now. (Source: Microsoft Security Response Center, August 2026)

Daily Tip

Open Settings, click System, then Notifications, and you'll see every app that's allowed to send you popups. Toggle off the apps that keep bothering you — game promotions, antivirus reminders, random app notifications. Scroll down to "Additional settings" and turn off "Get tips, tricks, and suggestions as you use Windows." Takes 30 seconds and your screen stays clean.
