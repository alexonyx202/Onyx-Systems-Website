Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Crooks are now posing as computer "rescue" companies to steal money. Researchers say a group calling itself Ransom Busters has been emailing people right after their computers are attacked — sometimes before the attack is even public — and offering to "recover" their files for a fee. Researchers believe the "rescuers" are the same crooks behind the attacks. If you get an email or call like this, don't pay, don't reply, and don't call the number in the message. Real help never comes from a stranger demanding money. *Source: BleepingComputer — August 19, 2026*

  • A healthcare billing company called CareCloud says hackers broke into its systems back in March and exposed information on 3.7 million people. Breach notices went out in late July — if you get one, watch for scam emails, texts, or calls pretending to be about your medical records. Source: BleepingComputer — August 19, 2026
  • Microsoft is looking into a problem with this month's Windows updates that stops some games from opening on some Windows 11 computers. If a game won't start after updating, don't reinstall anything — the fix will arrive through Windows Update. Source: BleepingComputer — August 20, 2026
  • ChatGPT was down Wednesday evening — people couldn't sign in or open their chats for a few hours. It's back up now, and there's no sign any passwords were taken. Source: BleepingComputer — August 19, 2026
