Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

That "Virus Detected" Popup With a Phone Number Is Always a Scam. You're browsing the web and suddenly a loud siren blares. A giant red box fills your screen: "WARNING: 47 VIRUSES FOUND. Your files are being stolen. Call Microsoft Support NOW: 1-888-XXX-XXXX." Here's the truth: Microsoft, Apple, and Google never put phone numbers in security warnings. Ever. That popup is just a webpage — a picture designed to scare you. It can't see your files. It can't hurt anything. What to do: Close the window. If it won't close, hold your power button for 15 seconds until the screen goes black, then turn it back on. Everything will be fine. The rule: If you see a phone number in an error message, it's a scam. Every single time. Source: FTC.gov, Microsoft Support — 2026

DAILY TIP

Hold down the Ctrl key (bottom-left of your keyboard) and tap the + key. Everything gets bigger. Ctrl and - makes it smaller. Ctrl and 0 resets it. Works everywhere — browsers, email, Word, File Explorer — every time.
