Your Daily Computer Brief

**Update Firefox and Chrome today — working exploit code is already public.** Mozilla confirmed on July 19 that public exploit code now exists for two critical Firefox flaws (CVE-2026-15718 and CVE-2026-15719). No attacks yet, but that window shuts fast once code is loose. Chrome also shipped emergency fixes the same week for 15 bugs, two of them critical. What to do: fully close Firefox and reopen it — that installs version 152.0.6. In Chrome, click the three-dot menu → Help → About Google Chrome to force the update now. Don't wait for the slow automatic rollout; it can take days. Source: Mozilla MFSA 2026-67; TechTimes, July 19, 2026; Malwarebytes, July 16, 2026

Daily Tip

Right-click an empty spot on your desktop, choose New → Folder, and name it "Bills 2026" or "Photos." Drag your loose files into it. Your desktop stays uncluttered, and you'll find things in seconds instead of digging through a pile.
