Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Thieves have been slipping a few lines of sneaky code onto the checkout pages of more than a hundred online shops, so every card number typed in gets quietly copied to them. Over 600,000 cards have been taken since July, according to security researchers. The shops look normal — you can't spot this scam by looking. Check your last card statement today and report anything you don't recognize. I've switched to a credit card for online shopping, never a debit card — it's far easier to get your money back. Source: BleepingComputer, Sept. 24, 2026, reporting research from security firm Gambit Security.
