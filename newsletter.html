🔴 ALERT

The FBI says scammers impersonating government officials have hit nearly 61,000 people since January 2025, stealing over $1.6 billion. They call or email pretending to be the FBI, IRS, Social Security, or local police — saying you owe money, your identity was stolen, or there's a warrant. They demand payment by wire, gift card, or crypto. What to do: Hang up. Government agencies never call demanding money or threatening arrest. If you're worried, look up the real number yourself and call back. Never pay a caller who says "act now or else." Why it matters: These scams work because they scare you. I've had customers who nearly paid before calling me. The FBI re-issued this warning in September because the losses keep climbing. Source: FBI IC3, FBI press release — September 17, 2026

Daily Tip

Once in a while, reach behind your computer and make sure every cable is snug — power, monitor, internet, keyboard, mouse. A loose cable causes flickering screens, "no internet" errors, and dead peripherals more often than you'd think. Takes thirty seconds.
