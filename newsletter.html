🔴 ALERT

That "Verify You're Human" Box Is a Trap — Here's How to Spot It You click a link and a familiar checkbox appears: "I'm not a robot." You check it, and suddenly the page says there's a problem — it asks you to press Windows+R, paste something, and hit Enter. Stop. That's not a CAPTCHA. It's a ClickFix scam. The checkbox silently copied a malicious command to your clipboard, and the "verification" is tricking you into running it yourself. Real CAPTCHAs never ask you to open Run, PowerShell, Terminal, or any system tool. They never ask you to paste and execute commands, download files, or enable browser notifications. If a verification screen pushes you outside your browser, close the tab immediately. If you only clicked the checkbox without pasting or running anything, your risk is low — just close the page and clear any notification permissions it may have asked for. If you did run a command, disconnect from the internet, run a full security scan, and change your important passwords from a device you know is safe. Source: Barracuda Networks, ESET — October 2026

Daily Tip

Hold down the Ctrl key (bottom-left corner of your keyboard) and tap the + key. Everything on screen gets bigger. Ctrl and − makes it smaller. Ctrl and 0 resets it. Works in your web browser, email, File Explorer, and most programs — every time, no settings to dig through.
