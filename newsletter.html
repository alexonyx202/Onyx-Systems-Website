🔴 ALERT

Your caller ID says "Federal Trade Commission." The voice sounds official, uses a real employee's name, claims your identity was stolen — or that they've recovered money you lost. They need you to verify info or pay a "processing fee." **The FTC doesn't work this way.** They never cold-call demanding payment. No gift cards, crypto, wire transfers, or gold. No "agents" with badge numbers — the FTC has warned repeatedly that anyone claiming to be an FTC agent with a badge is lying. What to do: Hang up. Share nothing. If concerned, call the FTC yourself at 1-877-FTC-HELP or visit ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Real agencies don't pressure you to act instantly — scammers do. *Source: FTC Consumer Alerts, Bitdefender HotforSecurity — July 2026*
