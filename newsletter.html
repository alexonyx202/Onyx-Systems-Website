🔴 ALERT

**FTC Warns: Fake CAPTCHA Scams Hijack Your Computer** You're browsing a website and suddenly a CAPTCHA appears — "Click to prove you're human." It looks normal: pick the traffic lights, type the squiggly letters. But this one is a trap. The FTC issued a consumer alert in June 2026: scammers are planting fake CAPTCHA boxes that trick you into running malicious commands on your own computer. Here's how it works: the fake CAPTCHA asks you to press `Win+R`, then paste a command, then hit Enter. You think you're verifying you're human. You're actually installing malware. Real CAPTCHAs *never* ask you to open Run boxes, paste commands, or use keyboard shortcuts. **What to do:** Close the browser tab immediately. If it won't close, hold your power button for 15 seconds to force a hard shutdown. Run a malware scan after restarting. *Source: FTC Consumer Alert, June 2026*

Daily Tip
