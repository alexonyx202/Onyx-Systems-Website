🔴 ALERT

Security researchers just caught criminals breaking into a popular brand of internet router and taking complete control — no password needed. If crooks get into your router, they can watch everything you do online and steer you to fake websites that steal your logins. Your best defense takes two minutes: unplug your router, count to ten, plug it back in, then check its settings for a software update — or call your internet company and ask them to update it for you. *Source: CERT Polska advisory (Sept. 5, 2026); SecurityAffairs (Sept. 6, 2026)*

Daily Tip

Open your email's settings and click the option called "two-step verification." Turn it on — a short code gets texted to you after your password. Two minutes of setup, and a stolen password alone can't get into your email.
