🔴 ALERT

Open enrollment season runs through January 15, 2027, and scammers are flooding inboxes and phones with fake health insurance offers. The FCC warns that robocalls and texts pretending to be from "Medicare," "your insurance provider," or "Healthcare.gov" spike during this window. They pressure you to "verify" your Social Security number or pay a fee to keep coverage. Real insurers and government agencies never ask for payment or personal info over an unsolicited call or text. What to do: Hang up. Delete the text. If you need to check your coverage, go directly to Healthcare.gov or call the number on your insurance card. Never use a number or link from the message. Source: FCC.gov — November 1, 2026
