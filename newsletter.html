Your Daily Computer Brief

That Text From "The FTC" Is a Lie Scammers are texting folks pretending to be FTC employees — and they'll send a photo of a fake government badge to look legit. Their pitch: they can recover money you lost in an earlier scam. It's a cruel twist that targets people who've already been burned. Here's the truth: a real FTC agent will never text you, never send a photo ID, and never ask you to move or pay money. If one lands in your messages, don't reply and don't pay. Forward the text to 7726 and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. *Source: FTC.gov/imposter — July 16, 2026*

Daily Tip

Drowning in junk newsletters? Scroll to the very bottom of one and click the Unsubscribe link — by law it has to be there. But if unsubscribing asks you to log in or pay, it's a scam. Just mark it as spam instead. Your inbox (and your temper) will thank you.
