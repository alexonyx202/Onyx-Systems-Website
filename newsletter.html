🔴 ALERT

Last Tuesday, Weber switched off the servers behind its June countertop oven. People paid up to $1,500, and now the app, the camera, and the guided cooking are gone — the oven only bakes. Nothing broke; the company just turned it off. Any gadget in your house that runs on the maker's servers can be next. The two-minute check: open the app for each smart device you own. If it cannot work without signing in to the company's account, that gadget has an expiration date. Source: The Verge (Sept. 22, 2026), reporting that Weber shut down the June Oven's cloud servers; the oven keeps working only for basic cooking.

Daily Tip

Before you install anything new, make a save point for the whole computer. Press the Start button, type Create a restore point, open it, and click Create. If a new app or an update later makes the machine act up, you can jump straight back to today — your pictures and documents stay exactly where they are.
