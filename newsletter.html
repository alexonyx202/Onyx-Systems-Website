🔴 ALERT

Scammers are using artificial intelligence to make charity appeals sound personal and urgent. A message may use a familiar organization’s name, a copied logo, or a convincing voice recording, then ask for gift cards, cryptocurrency, or a wire transfer. Before giving, stop and visit the organization’s official website by typing its address yourself. Never use payment instructions from an unexpected message. The Federal Trade Commission says pressure to pay immediately is a major warning sign. *Source: Federal Trade Commission — August 2026*

Daily Tip

If your printer is acting strange, press Start and type **Printers**, choose your printer, and open its settings. Select **Print a test page** before replacing ink or changing other settings. If the test page looks good, the problem is probably with the document or program—not the printer itself.
