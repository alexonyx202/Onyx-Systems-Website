Firefox 157.0.1 Adds Shield Warning for Encrypted Traffic Interception — Free at firefox.com Firefox 157.0.1 introduces a new shield icon that warns when software on your PC intercepts your encrypted traffic — including corporate monitoring tools and malware. Click the shield to see what's doing it. Most people have no idea their secure banking session can be read by something else on their computer. The fix is simple: install the update, check the shield, and remove anything you don't recognize. Here's the truth: your PC might be compromised in other ways — not just through the browser. When you install free software, always check Settings > Apps > Installed Apps after. Remove anything you didn't install yourself. And never ignore a security update.
