🔴 ALERT

**Scammers Are Pretending to Be the FBI's Internet Crime Center to Steal From You — Again.** You get a message: "We recovered your stolen money. Click here to claim it." The sender claims to be from the IC3 — the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center. They might even have an AI-generated video of an FBI official "confirming" it's real. It's a lie. The IC3 never contacts victims through social media, email, or phone. They never recover funds for you. They never ask for payment to release money. This is a re-victimization scam targeting people who've already been scammed once. What to do: Type www.ic3.gov directly into your browser — never click a link. If someone says they're from IC3 and wants money or info, it's a scam. Report it at the real IC3 site. *Source: FBI IC3 PSA — July 2026*
