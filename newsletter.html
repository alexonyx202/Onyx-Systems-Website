🔴 ALERT

Scammers are mailing official-looking postcards to veterans across the country, promising free monthly VA benefit payments. The postcard includes a phone number and website asking for your Social Security number or bank details to "verify eligibility." The real VA never contacts veterans this way — they already have your information on file. If you or a loved one receives one of these postcards, do not call the number or visit the website. Instead, verify any VA communication through va.gov or call 1-800-827-1000 directly. Report the scam to ReportFraud.ftc.gov. — Source: FTC, August 24, 2026

Daily Tip

Your smart TV watches what you watch — and sells that information to advertisers. Samsung, LG, and other brands use a feature called Automatic Content Recognition to track every show and movie. Turn it off: press the Settings button on your remote, go to Privacy or Preferences, look for "Smart TV Experience" or "Viewing Information Services," and switch it off. It takes 30 seconds and stops your viewing habits from being sold. The setting is buried in menus on purpose because TV companies make money from the data.
