🚨 This Week's Critical Alert: Windows Updates Are Breaking Things

Microsoft's October and November updates broke Wi‑Fi, printers, and accounting software — and the November update wouldn't even install on some computers.

If your work Wi‑Fi stopped connecting, a printer wouldn't add, or your accounting program started crashing: it's not your computer. It's the Windows updates.

What to do right now: Don't install updates the day they come out. Wait 3–4 days. If you already updated and something's broken, click here for the full report with step‑by‑step fixes.

🔴 ALERT

Thousands of routers and network switches have been hijacked in recent weeks. Attackers are exploiting known flaws in Zyxel GS1900 switches and SonicWall VPN devices — nearly 1,000 Zyxel switches compromised worldwide, and SonicWall's SSL-VPN authentication bypass is being actively abused. CISA added six actively exploited vulnerabilities to its must-patch list on November 10, including flaws in Cisco BroadWorks and Barracuda email gateways. What to do: Log into your router or switch admin page (usually 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1 in your web browser). Check for a firmware update and install it immediately. If your device is older than 5 years and no updates are available, replace it. Change the default admin password if you haven't already. Why it matters: A compromised router gives attackers access to everything on your network — passwords, banking, cameras, smart devices. They can redirect you to fake websites, steal data, or use your connection for attacks. Source: CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog — November 10, 2026; BleepingComputer — November 10, 2026 (Zyxel, SonicWall, Barracuda exploits)

Daily Tip

Too many pop-ups breaking your focus? Press the Windows key and type "notifications" — click "Notifications & actions settings." Turn off "Get notifications from apps and other senders" for a quiet day, or scroll down and toggle off just the noisy apps (like news, shopping, or games). This keeps the alerts you want and silences the rest.
