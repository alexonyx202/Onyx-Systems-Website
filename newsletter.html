Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Scammers are calling and texting people, pretending to be from the FCC — the government agency that watches over phones and the internet. According to the FCC, they say your phone number is under investigation for criminal activity and demand money or personal information to make it stop. That's a lie — no real agency does that. If you get this call, hang up. Don't call the number they give you, don't pay anything, and don't share personal information with anyone who calls out of the blue. *Source: FCC consumer alert — August 2026*

Daily Tip

Stop scrolling through months of email on your computer. Open your email program, click the search box at the top, type the sender's name or one word you remember, and press Enter — it appears in seconds. In Outlook, the search box is in the top bar. In webmail, look for the magnifying glass. You'll never dig through your inbox again.
