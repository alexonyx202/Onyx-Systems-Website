🔴 ALERT

Big news if you or anyone in your family ever had a DNA test. Baylor Genetics — a company that runs genetic tests for cancer risk, pregnancy screening, and rare diseases — just confirmed hackers stole personal information of 2.8 million people. We are talking names, Social Security numbers, medical diagnoses, and your actual genetic test results (source: KCRA/Hearst TV, Aug 28). If that sounds like you, check baylorgenetics.com/securityupdate right now to see if you were affected. Then place a free credit freeze at all three credit bureaus — Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion. Federal law makes credit freezes free, and it takes about five minutes per bureau.

Daily Tip

Your laptop touchpad already has superpowers — you just have not tried them yet. Swipe up with three fingers to see all your open windows at once. Swipe left or right with three fingers to jump between apps instantly. Pinch with three fingers to zoom in on anything. No install, no settings change, it just works on any modern Windows laptop. Give it two minutes today and you will wonder how you ever worked without it.
