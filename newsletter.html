Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Crooks are buying up expired website addresses — spending millions to do it — and turning them into traps that install bad programs or run scams. Every day, thousands of website addresses lapse, and security researchers say criminals snap them up to fool people who've trusted those sites for years. If a familiar website suddenly looks different, begs you to install something, or flashes alarming pop-ups, close it. Type the company's address yourself instead, and never click a link that pushes you to a site. *Source: Security Affairs — August 15, 2026*

Daily Tip

Can't quite read the words on your screen anymore? You don't need a new computer — Windows can make everything bigger in about 30 seconds. Right-click an empty spot on your desktop, choose Display settings, and drag the Scale slider up to 125% or 150%. Every word, button, and menu grows at once. It's built into Windows and free.
