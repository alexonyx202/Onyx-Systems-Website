🔴 ALERT

Microsoft fixed a dangerous Office flaw last month — opening a booby-trapped document could let someone run commands on your PC. The patch came through the regular update channel. Open Word or Excel, click File > Account > Update Options, and choose Update Now to confirm you're current. What to do: Open any Office app. Click File > Account > Update Options > Update Now. If it says you're up to date, you're protected. The rule: Malicious documents are one of the oldest tricks in the book. If you didn't expect a file, don't open it — even if it looks like it came from someone you know. Source: Microsoft Security Update Guide — October 2026

Daily Tip

Most routers still use the default admin password shown on the bottom sticker. Open a browser and go to 192.168.1.1 (or 192.168.0.1). Log in with "admin" and the sticker password, then change the admin password to something only you know. Takes two minutes and stops strangers from taking over your home network.
