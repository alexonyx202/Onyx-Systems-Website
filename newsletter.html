Microsoft's October and November updates broke Wi‑Fi, printers, and accounting software — and the November update wouldn't even install on some computers.

🔴 ALERT

Adobe just released its November 2026 security patches across nine product families including Photoshop, Acrobat, and Creative Cloud. Scammers know this and are pushing fake "Adobe update" pop-ups and emails that look identical to the real thing — same logos, same wording, same urgency. Clicking them installs malicious code instead of the actual patch. What to do: Never update Adobe apps from a pop-up or email link. Open the Creative Cloud desktop app directly, or go to adobe.com and sign in to check for updates. Real Adobe updates never come through browser pop-ups. Source: Adobe Security Bulletins APSB26-181, hkcert.org — November 2026

Daily Tip

Unwanted newsletters and promo emails clutter your inbox and make it hard to find what matters. Open your email, search for "unsubscribe" to find mailing lists, and click the unsubscribe link at the bottom of each one. Or create a simple filter to auto-sort promos into a separate folder. This keeps your inbox focused on what you actually need to see.
