🔴 ALERT

Scammers are impersonating the FBI's IC3 center, claiming they've "recovered" your stolen money and need remote access or banking logins to return it. The real FBI never calls out of the blue, never asks for remote access, passwords, or fees. Hang up. Don't click unexpected links. Report at ic3.gov. If you gave access or money, call your bank and bring your computer in. *Source: FBI Public Service Announcement — July 20, 2026*

Daily Tip

Press Ctrl+D to bookmark a page, then click the folder dropdown and choose "New folder" — name it something like "Shopping," "News," or "Work." Drag existing bookmarks into folders from the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B to show/hide). This keeps your bar clean and lets you find saved sites in seconds.
