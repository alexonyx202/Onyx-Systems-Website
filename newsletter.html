Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Security researchers found a new trick hitting over 4,500 companies across the U.S. Crooks send emails with attachments that look like a Microsoft login page. When you open the attachment, it loads a fake sign-in screen and steals your username, password, and even your two-factor code — all in one shot. The attackers then reuse your login session without needing your password again. The FTC says never open unexpected email attachments, even if they look official. If you get a surprise attachment asking you to "verify" your account, delete it and go directly to outlook.com or office.com instead (The Hacker News, August 25; CybersecurityNews, August 26).

Daily Tip

Your computer lets you choose exactly which apps are allowed to bother you with pop-ups. Press the Windows key, type "Notifications," and press Enter. You'll see a list of every app — scroll through and turn off the ones you don't need alerts from, like games, weather apps, or anything that spams you. You can also scroll down to find "Focus assist" and turn it on to silence everything while you work. Takes about two minutes and makes your computer way calmer.
