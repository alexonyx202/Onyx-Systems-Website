Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

A massive database of 16 billion stolen usernames and passwords is circulating on hacker forums right now. Researchers at Cybernews found it — a compilation of breaches from LinkedIn, Adobe, MySpace, and hundreds of other sites going back years. If you've reused a password anywhere, it's in there. Here's what to do: Go to haveibeenpwned.com and type in your email. It'll tell you which breaches caught you. Then change the password on every account that used the same one. Use a different password for each site. A password manager makes this painless — Bitwarden, 1Password, or the one built into your browser. Why it matters: Criminals take these lists and try the same email/password combo on banking sites, email, shopping — anywhere money or data lives. They automate it. One reused password is all they need. Source: Cybernews "16 Billion Credentials Exposed in Largest Compilation Yet" — July 2026; Have I Been Pwned breach database
