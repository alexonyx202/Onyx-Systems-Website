Your Daily Computer Brief

A dangerous new malware framework named Avalon is spreading through plain-looking phishing emails. Once it's opened, it can steal your passwords, move through your home network, and lock your files for ransom — it's packaged with CrownX ransomware. I see this kind of attack get sneakier every year. The rule hasn't changed: never open attachments or click links in unexpected emails, even if they look legit. Type the web address in yourself. (Source: The Hacker News, July 2026)

Today in Tech

  • Microsoft shipped an emergency Windows 11 fix (KB5121767) on July 19 after some Intel PCs randomly shut down or ran hot. Check for updates if yours is acting up. (Source: Windows Latest)
  • Password-manager users: a fresh LastPass phishing wave uses lookalike domains (lastpassnewsletter[.]com) urging you to "accept updated terms." LastPass never asks for your master password by email. (Source: Forbes, Jul 16)
  • Microsoft's July Patch Tuesday patched a record 570+ flaws, including two zero-days already exploited in attacks. If you haven't updated Windows this month, do it today. (Source: BleepingComputer)

Daily Tip

If your internet keeps dropping on one computer, forget the network and rejoin it. Click the Wi-Fi icon (bottom-right), right-click your network, choose "Forget," then reconnect and re-enter the password. Takes two minutes and clears most mystery disconnects.
