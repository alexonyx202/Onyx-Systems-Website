Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Google just patched Chrome's seventh actively exploited zero-day of 2026. That's seven times this year that attackers found a hole in Chrome before Google did — and used it against real people. The flaw (vulnerability) lives in Chrome's V8 JavaScript engine. Visit a malicious website and the code runs on your computer — no clicks, no downloads, just loading the page. Google confirmed active exploitation. CISA added it to their "must-patch-now" list. What to do: Open Chrome. Click the three dots (top right) > Help > About Google Chrome. It checks and updates automatically. Restart when prompted. Takes 30 seconds. Do it today. The rule: Your browser is the #1 way bad code gets on your computer. Keeping it updated is the single most effective protection you have. Source: Google Chrome Releases, CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities — September–October 2026
