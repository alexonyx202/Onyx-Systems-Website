Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

The FTC is warning about a cruel new twist: scammers buy lists of people who already lost money to fraud, then call or email pretending to be a government agency, a law firm, or a consumer group. They promise to get your money back — then demand an upfront "fee" or your bank information first. Real organizations never charge to return your money. If anyone asks you to pay to recover money, it's a scam. Hang up, don't pay, and report it free at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Source: FTC Consumer Alert — August 2026

Daily Tip

If the words on your screen are getting hard to read, don't squint — make them bigger. Click the Start button (bottom-left corner), click Settings (the gear icon), then Accessibility, then Text size. Drag the slider to the right and watch everything grow. It's free, built into Windows, and your eyes will thank you.
