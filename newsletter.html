Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

School's back, and scammers are working the season. Crooks set up fake online stores advertising laptops at prices too good to be true — they take your money and card number, then vanish. Others send emails that look like they're from your child's school, asking you to log in or pay a fee. Real schools never do that. Type the store's web address yourself instead of clicking a link, and call the school using the number you already have if anything feels off. *Source: Vision Bank consumer alert — August 14, 2026*

Today in Tech

  • Microsoft is quietly stopping new features for Word, Excel, and OneDrive on older Windows computers. Yours will keep working, but it's a sign your computer is getting older. Source: Windows Latest — August 13, 2026
  • Security researchers warn crooks are hijacking hotel Wi-Fi to send travelers to fake sign-in pages that steal passwords. Never type your email password into a hotel sign-in page. Source: KnowBe4 — August 14, 2026
  • The free video player VLC says a Windows bug is making it run slow. If your video player drags, make sure your updates are current. Source: Windows Latest — August 14, 2026
