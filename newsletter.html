🔴 ALERT

The FTC is warning about fake QR codes — the square black-and-white patterns you scan to pay for parking. Scammers print their own codes and stick them over the real ones on parking meters. Scan the wrong one and it opens a fake payment page that steals your card number. Before you pay, check the code: if it looks like a sticker slapped over the real sign, don't scan it. Type the parking service's web address yourself, pay at the machine, and report fakes at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Source: Federal Trade Commission — September 3, 2026

Daily Tip

Pick a folder and let your email do the moving: right-click a newsletter in your inbox, choose "Create rule" (or "Filter messages like these"), and tell it to file matching mail into a folder you name — "Newsletters," "Receipts." Two minutes once, and your inbox stays short from then on.
