🔴 ALERT

Character.AI — the popular AI chatbot platform — just filed for bankruptcy today. Scammers will jump on this immediately. Expect fake emails claiming to be from "Character.AI Support" about account closures, data retrieval, or refunds. They'll include links that steal your login credentials. Here's the rule: Companies in bankruptcy don't email you asking for passwords. If you get an unexpected email from any service asking you to "verify your account" or "click here to save your data," it's a trap. What to do: Don't click any links in the email. If you're worried, open your browser and type the website address yourself. Real companies never ask for passwords via email. Source: TechCrunch — October 13, 2026
