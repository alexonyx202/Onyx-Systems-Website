Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Scammers just fooled the online bank Revolut into handing over private files for about 50,000 customers — birth dates, addresses, phone numbers, copies of passports and driver's licenses, even the selfies taken to verify accounts. How? They posed as a government agency using a real government email address, so the request looked completely legitimate. Expect very convincing follow-up scams that quote your real details. Don't trust caller ID or an official-looking email: hang up and call the company back at the number on your card or statement. *Source: Reuters and TechCrunch (Sept. 12–14, 2026)*

Daily Tip

Company coming and nobody remembers the Wi-Fi password? On your Windows PC, click Start (bottom-left corner), type Wi-Fi settings, and press Enter. Click your network's name, then the little arrow beside it, and click Show next to Password. Windows asks you to confirm — click Yes, and the password appears on screen. No calling the internet company.
