Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Scammers built a fake website that looks exactly like the download page for CCleaner, a popular free cleanup program. People who downloaded it got a bad program hiding on their computer that could watch everything they typed and steal passwords and bank logins. The same trick is spreading with fake copies of 7-Zip and Adobe programs. Only download software from the company's official website — check the web address at the very top of your screen first. If you're not sure, close the page and ask me. *Source: Malwarebytes — August 2026*

Today in Tech

  • A free AI program that companies use was quietly poisoned, and security researchers say it leaked terabytes of passwords and sign-in details before anyone caught it. Another good reason to use a long, different password for every account. Source: Ars Technica — August 12, 2026
  • A security researcher just published a new way to break into Microsoft's built-in Defender protection that works even on fully updated computers. Microsoft says it's working on a fix — keep your updates on and be careful what you download. Source: BleepingComputer — August 12, 2026

Daily Tip

Copy something, copy something else, and your first copy is usually gone. Windows can remember everything you've copied for you. Press the Windows key and the letter V at the same time, and click "Turn on" when it asks. From then on, Win+V shows everything you copied recently so you can paste any of it. Free and built into Windows.
