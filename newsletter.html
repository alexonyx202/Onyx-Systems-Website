Your Daily Computer Brief

🔴 ALERT

Windows 10 support ends **October 14, 2026** — six weeks from today. After that date Microsoft stops releasing security updates for Windows 10, so newly discovered vulnerabilities won't be patched on your PC. Check which version you have now: press **Start**, type **About your PC**, and look at the **Windows specifications** section. If it says Windows 10, the free upgrade to Windows 11 is still available for most machines — or it's time to plan a replacement. Not sure? Bring it in for a free check; don't wait until October. *Source: Microsoft — Windows 10 End of Support (October 14, 2026)*

Daily Tip

Before moving from Windows 10 to Windows 11, back up your files — it turns the upgrade into a non-event. Copy your **Documents**, **Pictures**, and **Desktop** folders to an external drive or cloud storage (Settings → Update & Security → Backup). Upgrades usually go smoothly, but a backup means even a worst-case scenario costs you nothing.
