━━━ 🔴 ALERT ━━━

Your phone rings. The caller says they're from Microsoft Support (or
Windows, or Apple). They claim your computer has a virus, or your
license expired, or hackers are stealing your files right now. They
sound professional. They may even know your name.

Here's the truth: Microsoft, Apple, and Google never call you out of
the blue. Never. If you see a phone number in a popup or get an
unsolicited "support" call, it's a scam. Every single time.

What to do: hang up. Don't press any buttons. Don't give them remote
access to your computer. Don't read them numbers from your screen.
Just hang up. If you're worried about your computer, call a local shop
you trust — or bring it in.

Source: FTC.gov Consumer Alerts — October 2026
