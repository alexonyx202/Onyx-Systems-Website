━━━ 🔴 ALERT ━━━

Your phone rings. It sounds exactly like your grandson — panicked, saying he's been in a car accident and arrested. He needs bail money. A "public defender" gets on the line and tells you to put cash in an envelope. Then a courier shows up at your front door to collect it.

The voice was AI-generated. The accident never happened. The courier works for the scammers. This happened to an Orlando woman last month — she lost thousands in cash.

What to do: Hang up. Call your family member directly on their known number. No legitimate emergency requires cash handed to a stranger at your door. The FBI warns this "courier scam" is rising, and AI voice cloning makes it terrifyingly convincing.

Source: F-Secure F-Alert November 2026, FBI Boston field office warning — October 2026

━━━ 💡 DAILY TIP: Spot a Fake Login Page ━━━

Before you type your password, check the address bar. The real site will show the exact name (like "google.com" or "bankofamerica.com") with a lock icon. A fake page often has extra words ("google-security-login.com") or a slightly wrong spelling. If it looks off, close it and type the address yourself.
