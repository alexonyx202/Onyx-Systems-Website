━━━ 🔴 ALERT ━━━

Text messages claiming "Your tax refund is ready — click here to claim" are hitting phones across North Florida right now. The link goes to a fake IRS page that steals your Social Security number, date of birth, and bank info. The IRS never texts or emails you a link to claim a refund. They send letters by mail. Period.

What to do: Delete the text. Don't click the link. If you're expecting a refund, check directly at irs.gov/refunds or call the IRS at 800-829-1040. Report the scam text to phishing@irs.gov and forward the text to 7726 (SPAM).

*Source: IRS.gov "Tax Scams" consumer alert — October 2026; FTC.gov "IRS Impersonation Scams" — 2026*

━━━ 💡 DAILY TIP: Pin Your Most-Used Program to the Taskbar ━━━

Right-click the program's icon in the Start menu (bottom-left corner) and choose "Pin to taskbar." It stays there permanently — one click to open, no hunting through menus. I pin my browser, email, and word processor.
