━━━ 🔴 ALERT ━━━

Holiday charity scams are flooding email and social media right now. Scammers create fake charities with names that sound like real ones — "Children's Cancer Fund" vs. "Children's Cancer Foundation." They use urgent language: "Disaster victims need help today." They ask for payment by gift card, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency. Real charities never ask for those.

What to do: Before donating, check the charity at give.org (BBB Wise Giving Alliance) or charitynavigator.org. Search the exact name plus "scam" or "review." Pay by credit card on the charity's official website — not through a link in an email or social media post.

Why it matters: Americans lost billions to charity fraud last year. The money goes to criminals, not people in need. Once you send a gift card or wire transfer, it's gone forever. A two-minute check protects your generosity.

*Source: FTC Consumer Advice — November 2026; BBB Wise Giving Alliance — November 2026*

━━━ 💡 DAILY TIP: Back Up Your Important Files ━━━

Plug in a USB drive or external hard drive. Open File Explorer (Windows key + E). Drag your Documents, Pictures, and Desktop folders to the drive. Do this once a month. If ransomware hits or a drive fails, your irreplaceable photos and documents are safe on the backup.
