━━━ 🔴 ALERT ━━━

Hurricane season runs through November, and scammers know it. After every storm, fake charity websites, crowdfunding campaigns, and phone calls flood in — all pretending to help victims.

They'll pressure you to donate right now. They'll ask for gift cards, wire transfers, or cryptocurrency. Real charities never ask for those. They'll use names that sound almost like real organizations (e.g., "Florida Hurricane Relief Fund" vs. the real "Florida Disaster Fund").

What to do: Before donating, verify the charity at ftc.gov/charity or guidestar.org. Pay by credit card only — it has fraud protection. If someone calls pushing for immediate payment, hang up.

I've had two customers this month who nearly fell for this. The urgency tactic works because you want to help.

Source: FTC Consumer Alert, Alachua Chronicle — August/October 2026
