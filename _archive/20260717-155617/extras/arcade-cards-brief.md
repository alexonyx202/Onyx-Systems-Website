# ARCADE Card Redesign — Best-Practices Brief

## (a) Design verdict
The cards read as "text with a screenshot bolted on" because the screenshot occupies <50% of card height and the title+tagline+button footer is taller than the visual — fix by making the cover image the dominant ~65% of the card, pinning the button to the bottom, and cropping each 16:9 shot to its action focal point.

## (b) Enforce these numeric rules in CSS
1. **Image-to-text ratio: cover = 62–68% of card height, text footer ≤38%.** Best-in-class stores are image-led: Steam capsules and Google Play feature graphics are ~100% image (title overlaid), not a separate text block. A 5-game grid card should give the screenshot majority share. Target: image height ≈ 0.65 × card height. (Steam capsules: 460×215 / 616×353 — image-only; Google Play feature graphic 1024×500 — image-only. [Steam](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets), [Google Play](https://screenkit.tools/specs/google-play-feature-graphic-size))
2. **Keep preview aspect-ratio 16:9** (matches existing webp assets and reads as a "game preview slide" — it is the mandated video/game ratio: CrazyGames landscape cover is 1920×1080/16:9; Steam/Play banners ≈2:1). Do NOT switch to 1:1 or 4:5 — those are app-icon/square-tile ratios (Poki square 628×628), which read as "avatar," not "gameplay." If you want MORE image dominance, widen to ~2:1 (Steam header 920×430 = 2.14:1). [CrazyGames](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/game-covers/)
3. **Uniform height via flex, not fragile CSS.** `.game-card{display:flex;flex-direction:column;height:340px}` (or fixed height). `.gthumb{aspect-ratio:16/9;width:100%}` so the image box is identical on every card. `.gbody{flex:1;display:flex;flex-direction:column}` and the "▶ Play now" button gets `margin-top:auto` to pin to the bottom. Reserve `min-height` on the title (e.g. 2 lines ≈ 2.6em) so one long title doesn't push others out of alignment. [Material Design cards](https://m3.material.io/components/cards/guidelines)
4. **object-fit:cover, never contain.** `cover` fills the frame and crops (no letterbox, no distortion); `contain` leaves empty bars that are exactly the "weak" look we're killing. Use `object-position:50% 40%` to bias the crop toward the action/focal point (eyes read upper-middle first). [MDN object-fit](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/object-fit)
5. **Use composed key-art / title screen, not raw gameplay capture.** CrazyGames explicitly says "Don't just take a screenshot… be more creative" — use a start screen or hero composition with the game title baked in. Avoid live gameplay capture (rAF/continuous-render loops waste CPU, throttle poorly, and look noisy at small sizes). Poki: "Avoid text in thumbnail" for tiles, but a static title baked into key-art is fine at card scale and aids recognition. [Poki](https://sdk.poki.com/game-thumbnail), [CrazyGames](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/game-covers/)
6. **Gradient scrim for legibility.** Overlay a bottom-up linear-gradient `rgba(10,8,20,0)→rgba(10,8,20,.85)` on the lower ~30% of the image if the title sits on the image; otherwise keep the title in the footer on the solid neon card background. [NN/g cards: clear hierarchy](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/cards-component/)
7. **WCAG-AA contrast.** Body/title text ≥ 4.5:1 vs background; large text (≥18pt or 14pt bold, i.e. your title) ≥ 3:1. On a dark synthwave bg use near-white (#F2F2F7) or neon-cyan (#5EF2FF) titles — both clear 4.5:1+ over #0B0A14. Test the worst point of any gradient/scrim. [W3C 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [MDN contrast](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Accessibility/Guides/Understanding_WCAG/Perceivable/Color_contrast)
8. **Typography & motion.** Title: 1.05–1.2rem, weight 700, single line + ellipsis. Tagline: keep ≤1 line (drop to a short 1-line subtitle or remove if redundant with title). Hover: lift card `translateY(-4px)` + brighten neon border, `transition:200ms ease`; image `scale(1.03)` over 300ms. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (disable transforms). Keep micro-interactions ≤200–300ms — the more frequent, the subtler. [NN/g motion duration](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-duration/)
9. **Chrome restraint.** One neon bezel + one marquee-style label per card max. A CRT/scanline overlay is acceptable only as a very low-opacity (≤6%) repeating-linear-gradient; skip it if it muddies the screenshot. Clean > cluttered: the game art should be the star, not the frame. (Poki/CrazyGames both warn over-decoration hurts CTR.)
