## Post 5

**Headline:** Fake Antivirus Renewal Emails Steal Your Credit Card — Check Subscription Status Directly in the App

**The Tip:** If an email says your Norton or McAfee subscription renewed for $80–$100, don't click the link. Open the security app on your computer or go to norton.com / mcafee.com directly to check your real status.

**The Shop Owner's Truth:** This scam works because antivirus renewals are confusing — the product came with the PC, someone else manages it, or you forgot. The email exploits that uncertainty. I see two customers a month who nearly paid.

**What I Actually Do:** Open the installed antivirus (Windows Security, Norton, McAfee, Bitdefender — whatever's there), click Account or Subscription, verify the date and price. If it's fine, delete the email. If it's expiring, renew in the app, not the email.

**The Onyx Promise:** If you're not sure, forward the email to me or bring the computer in. Free check, no judgment.
