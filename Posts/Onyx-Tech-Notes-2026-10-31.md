It works offline for English and works on any video, podcast, or meeting. No upload, no account, no cost. Free at microsoft.com/windows/tips/live-captions or just press Win+Ctrl+L.

**The Shop Owner's Truth:** Most people don't realize this feature exists — it's been in Windows 11 since 2022 and keeps getting better. I show it to customers who struggle to hear videos or watch content in noisy environments, and they're always surprised it's free and offline.

**What I Actually Do:** Press Win+Ctrl+L on any Windows 11 PC. First run downloads a small speech model (about 30 MB). After that it works completely offline. Settings > Accessibility > Captions lets you customize position, style, and language.

## Post 3

**Headline:** Windows 11 25H2 and 26H2 are the new supported versions — free upgrade at Settings > Windows Update if you are on 24H2 Home or Pro

**The Tip:** Windows 11 24H2 Home and Pro reached end of updates October 13 2026. Microsoft now supports 25H2 and 26H2 with monthly security patches. The upgrade is free — go to Settings > Windows Update and check for updates. If your PC says it's up to date on 24H2 you need to manually trigger the version upgrade. Source: Microsoft Support KB5127648 October 13 2026

**The Shop Owner's Truth:** I've had customers bring in PCs that "work fine" but haven't had a security patch in months. Windows won't always offer the version upgrade automatically — you have to click "Check for updates" and sometimes toggle "Get the latest updates as soon as they're available" in Advanced options.

**What I Actually Do:** Settings > Windows Update > Advanced options > turn on "Get the latest updates as soon as they're available" > Check for updates. If 26H2 doesn't appear, download the Installation Assistant from microsoft.com/software-download/windows11. Takes 20-30 minutes, single restart.

## Post 5

**Headline:** One click in Windows Security turns on free ransomware protection — Controlled Folder Access blocks unauthorized encryption

**The Tip:** Windows 11 has a free ransomware shield built in called Controlled Folder Access. It stops unknown apps from encrypting your documents, pictures, and desktop folders. Open Windows Security (type "Windows Security" in Start), click Virus & threat protection, then Manage ransomware protection, and turn on Controlled Folder Access. Takes 30 seconds. I enable this on every customer computer. Source: Microsoft Support, How-To Geek, Pureinfotech

**The Shop Owner's Truth:** Ransomware doesn't care if you're a "small target." It's automated — it hits everyone. Controlled Folder Access is the one setting that actually stops the encryption from happening. It's free, already installed, and I turn it on for every single customer. Most people have never heard of it.

**What I Actually Do:** Start > type "Windows Security" > Enter > Virus & threat protection > Manage ransomware protection > turn on Controlled Folder Access. If a legitimate app gets blocked (rare), click "Allow an app through Controlled folder access" and add it. Done.
