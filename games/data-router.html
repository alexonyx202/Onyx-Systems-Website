INSTRUCTIONS

MISSION ▸ Get the packet from the START square up to the CPU CORES and land it dead-centre in a socket bay — the gaps between ports are open water. Fill all five to move up a level. Beat the clock!

MOVE ▸ ↑↓←→ or WASD to hop one tile. Swipe on touch screens.

THE ROAD ▸ Dodge the PINGS, STREAMS and big SERVERS. One hit and your packet is scrap. No free ride after a crash — you're back on the line with no shield.

BUS LINES ▸ Hop on the BUSES to cross the water. Miss one and you're fried. Watch out — some buses SINK!

BOOSTERS ▸ FIREWALL = one free hit · +TIME = more seconds · 1UP = extra packet · OVERCLOCK = fast hops · THROTTLE = slow traffic · BURST = smash through cars for +150 each

COMBOS ▸ Dock cores back-to-back to build a combo. Every 25,000 points earns an extra packet.

PAUSE ▸ P / ESC · MUTE ▸ M

GAMEPAD ▸ D-PAD / stick to hop · A confirm · B back · START pause · SELECT mute

DAILY ▸ A new fixed board every day, seeded from the date. Clear all five cores for the completion bonus and claim your rank on the daily leaderboard.

RUSH HOUR ▸ Every 20 seconds traffic DOUBLES for 6 seconds. Survive the surge for a +250 bonus — or pop a THROTTLE and slip through.
