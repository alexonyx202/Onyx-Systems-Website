HOW TO PLAY

Run the neon grid as far as you can. The city accelerates — react faster, and don't crash.

SERVER — jump over it (tap again mid-air to double-jump).
LASER GATE — too tall to jump: slide underneath.
DRONE — a hovering sentry. Time your jump to clear it.

⚡ Energy coins add distance — chain them for a combo. Graze an obstacle for a CLOSE CALL bonus. Survive to unlock achievements.

Practice is safe — no crashes. Pass all three drills.

CONTROLS

TOUCH

Left side — tap / hold / swipe ↑ to jump

Tap again mid-air — double jump

Right side — press to slide, release to stand

Swipe ↓ (right side) — slide

2 fingers ↑ / ↓ — jump / slide

KEYBOARD

Space / ↑ — jump

↓ / S — slide

P / Esc — pause

M — mute

F — fullscreen · R — restart
