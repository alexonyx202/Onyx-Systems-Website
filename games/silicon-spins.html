HOW TO PLAYSYSTEM MANUAL v3.1.4
OBJECTIVE. Line up 3 or more matching symbols on consecutive reels starting from the leftmost reel. With a 5×3 grid there are 243 ways to win — paylines don't matter, only matching runs from the left edge.
WAYS COUNT. A winning run's ways = the number of matching symbols on each reel, multiplied across the reels of the run. A run of 2 symbols × 2 symbols × 1 symbol = 4 ways. Each way pays the per-way rate shown below, times your bet.
RAM ON REELS 1–3 · 2 × 2 × 1 = 4 WAYS · EACH WAY PAYS THE RATE × BET
HOW TO SPIN. Choose a bet with − + or MAX, then hit SPIN (or press SPACE). While the reels spin, SPIN becomes STOP — press it (or SPACE) to settle the reels early; during autoplay, STOP also halts the run. TURBO (in Settings) shortens reel animation for faster play. AUTO opens a stop-conditions panel — choose a spin limit, stop on any win, stop on bonus trigger, or a win threshold. It stops early if the balance runs low. ESC pauses; ← → adjust the bet.
VIRTUAL CREDITS. You start with 10,000 virtual credits. Every base spin costs your current bet. Wins are added to your balance. If you hit zero, the system offers a free REBOOT WITH 10,000 CREDITS reset. Free spins never cost anything.
