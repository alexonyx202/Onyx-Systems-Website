System Status: CRITICAL

Deep inside the orbital server plexus, something has gone terribly wrong. Corrupted files multiply in the dark, cache tables choke on junk, and a swarm of hostile data — viruses, malware, logic bombs — has overrun the core.

You are a System Technician, last engineer on the line. Piloting a Tech Maintenance Ship, you must fire Data Scrubbers into the corrupted grids, shatter every overloaded block, and restore system integrity before the whole network collapses.

Eight corrupted bosses guard the deepest sectors. Purge them all and reboot the system.

Sector by sector. Block by block. Clean it all.

How to Play

Controls

  • Mouse / Touch: move ship, tap to launch
  • Arrows / A-D: move ship
  • Space: launch / fire
  • P / Esc: pause
  • M: music   N: sfx
  • Gamepad: supported

      Tips

      • Hitting the ship at the edges launches steep angles — aim with the edges.
      • Chain hits without dropping a ball to raise your combo multiplier.
      • Virus blocks and logic bombs are dangerous — purge them fast.
      • Don't let the ball fall: a lost ball resets your combo.
