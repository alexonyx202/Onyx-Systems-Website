HOW TO PLAY

You are the barrel. The river carries you downstream. Fish leap out of the water — rock them out of the sky with your slingshot.

THE SLINGSHOT

Like a real slingshot, the fork points at your target and the pouch pulls back — never toward the fish. The white crosshair marks where the rock will land, and the slingshot auto-aims: point the crosshair at a fish, and it fires with exactly the right speed and arc to hit it. No power to manage — just aim and release.

Your hands do one job at a time: while you're paddling, the slingshot is stowed and you can't shoot (the crosshair dims). Let go of the paddle to draw and fire.

DESKTOP

PRESS & DRAG aim the crosshair · RELEASE fire (the shot auto-aims at your crosshair)
AD or ←→ paddle to the banks · SPACE fire · ESC/P pause

GAMEPAD

LEFT STICK paddle · RIGHT STICK aim (push toward the fish) · RT/A hold & release to fire · START pause

TOUCH

Drag left half of the screen: paddle (virtual stick).
Drag right half: aim the crosshair — the pouch pulls back opposite the fish. Release to fire.

RULES

You start with a pouch of rocks. Hit fish to recover rocks — puffer and golden fish drop extra. Paddle to rock piles on the banks to restock. Hungry chompers will bite rocks out of your barrel — shoot them first! Steer clear of driftwood — bumping a log knocks rocks loose. Watch the river: it bends and narrows, rapids hide boulders, and the waterfall launches you over the falls for a rock bonus. Hit the end of the river with rocks left to clear the round and face THE BIG ONE. Run out of rocks and it's a swim home. Paddling and shooting can't happen at once — plan your shots between strokes. Chain hits for combo multipliers up to x9.
